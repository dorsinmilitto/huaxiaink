**Mount Emei** (峨眉山) is one of China's [Four Sacred Buddhist Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Sacred_Buddhist_Mountains), a 3,099-meter peak in Sichuan Province where Buddhism, biodiversity, and cloud-shrouded scenery converge into one of China's most profound hiking experiences.

## The Buddhist Legacy

Emei's Buddhist history dates to the 1st century CE, when the monk Puxian is said to have arrived on a white elephant — an event commemorated by the massive bronze **Puxian statue** on the Golden Summit. The mountain became a center of [Chinese Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Buddhism), with over 100 temples built across its slopes over two millennia.

The most significant include **Baoguo Temple** at the base, **Qingyin Pavilion** at the halfway point, and the **Golden Summit** (*Jinding*, 金顶) — the pilgrimage destination at 3,077 meters where a towering golden stupa rises above a sea of clouds.

## The Monkey Guardians

Emei's most famous — and notorious — residents are the **Tibetan macaques** (*Macaca thibetana*). These bold primates have learned that hikers carry food, and they are not shy about taking it. Monkey encounters are a rite of passage: they snatch bags, unzip backpacks, and occasionally sit on the trail demanding tribute.

Locals call them the "mountain's security guards." Buddhist pilgrims see them as incarnations of mountain spirits. Pragmatic hikers carry a [small daypack](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H6GWV4YD?tag=huaxiaink-20) with valuables secured and food in ziplocked pockets.

## The Sea of Clouds

The Golden Summit's defining spectacle is the **sea of clouds** (云海). When conditions align, the mountain peak rises above a blanket of white cloud, with only distant peaks poking through like islands. At dawn, the sun rises behind a **Buddha's Halo** — a circular rainbow formed by light refracting through mist — considered an auspicious sign.

This phenomenon is not guaranteed. Many pilgrims wait days for the clouds to clear. When it happens, the sight of golden light flooding the bronze Puxian statue above an ocean of cloud is genuinely transcendent.

## The Hiking Pilgrimage

The full hike from Baoguo Temple to the Golden Summit covers approximately 60 kilometers of stone steps — a two-to-three-day journey through five vegetation zones, from subtropical forest at the base to alpine meadow near the top. A cable car from Jieyin Hall provides a shortcut for those who want the summit without the climb.

Nearby, the [Leshan Giant Buddha](/en/travel/leshan-giant-buddha/) — the world's largest stone Buddha carved into a cliff face — is an easy day trip from the mountain's base.
